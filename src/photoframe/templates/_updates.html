During an update

Your photos, settings and schedule are kept. The displayed image remains on the frame while Photoframe restarts. This page reconnects automatically. If the new version cannot start, the updater restores the previous version.

Recovery and diagnostics

No update in progress.

If reconnection fails, wait a few minutes, then choose Reconnect. If the device remains unavailable, ask your administrator to inspect the photoframe and photoframe-updater service logs.

Apply update and restart?
