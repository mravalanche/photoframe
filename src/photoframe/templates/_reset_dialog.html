Reset & recovery

Reset Photoframe?

This clears your saved Immich key, album choice, frame settings and local photo cache. Photoframe returns to HTTP at 127.0.0.1:8000 and may restart. You’ll need your Immich URL and API key to set it up again. The app and system service stay installed.
